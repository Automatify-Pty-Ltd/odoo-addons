Social Publisher LinkedIn

LinkedIn publishing for Social Publisher on Odoo 19 Community

Connect a LinkedIn Developer App, authorize a personal profile or Company Page, and publish text posts through LinkedIn's official API from the Social Publisher workflow.

Features

  • Three-legged LinkedIn OAuth Authorization Code flow.
  • Personal Profile and Company Page author modes.
  • Company Page eligibility validation through LinkedIn organization access APIs.
  • Text post publishing through the versioned LinkedIn Posts API.
  • Integration with core scheduling, retries, and per-channel publication status.
  • One-time, expiring OAuth state bound to the initiating Odoo user and account.
  • Actionable account health on expired or rejected authorization.

Requirements and external service

Requires Social Publisher and a LinkedIn Developer App owned or authorized by the installing organization. The app must have the LinkedIn products and permissions required for the selected author mode. LinkedIn controls API access, approval, limits, and availability.

Client credentials and OAuth tokens are not bundled with this addon. The connector communicates directly with LinkedIn's official OAuth and API endpoints.
