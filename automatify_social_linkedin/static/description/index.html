Features

  • Three-legged LinkedIn OAuth Authorization Code flow.
  • Personal Profile and Company Page author modes.
  • Personal Profiles use LinkedIn OpenID Connect plus Share on LinkedIn permissions.
  • Company Pages use the publishing-only w_organization_social permission and an explicit organization Author URN supplied by the manager.
  • Eligible Company Page publishing roles such as Content Admin do not require an administrator-only organization ACL scope.
  • Provider-safe text publishing through the versioned LinkedIn Posts API.
  • Single-image JPG, PNG, or GIF publishing through LinkedIn Images + Posts APIs.
  • External post ID and LinkedIn link stored on the publication target.
  • Integration with core scheduling, retries, and per-channel publication status.
  • One-time, expiring OAuth state bound to the initiating Odoo user and account.

Setup

  1. Create or use your own LinkedIn Developer App.
  2. For Personal Profile publishing, enable Share on LinkedIn and Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect. Company Page publishing requires the LinkedIn organization publishing access needed for w_organization_social.
  3. Open Social Marketing → Configuration → LinkedIn Settings.
  4. Enter your own LinkedIn Client ID and Client Secret.
  5. Copy the OAuth Redirect URL displayed by Odoo into the exact Redirect URLs for your LinkedIn app.
  6. Create a LinkedIn Social Account. For a Company Page, enter the exact Author URN such as urn:li:organization:123456 before connecting.
  7. Choose Connect LinkedIn and approve the requested permissions.

Company Pages are not auto-discovered. After a Social Account is connected, its remote profile or Page identity remains bound to that Odoo record; create another Social Account to publish as a different identity.

The redirect URL is generated from the installation's standard Odoo web.base.url. No production callback URL, client credential, or OAuth token is bundled with this addon.
