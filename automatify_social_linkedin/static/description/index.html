Social Publisher LinkedIn

LinkedIn publishing for Social Publisher on Odoo 19 Community

Connect a LinkedIn Developer App, authorize a personal profile or Company Page, compose in Odoo, and publish provider-safe text with an optional image through LinkedIn's official APIs.

Features

  • Three-legged LinkedIn OAuth Authorization Code flow.
  • Personal Profile and Company Page author modes.
  • Company Page eligibility validation through LinkedIn organization access APIs.
  • Provider-safe text publishing through the versioned LinkedIn Posts API.
  • Single-image JPG, PNG, or GIF publishing through LinkedIn Images + Posts APIs.
  • External post ID and convenient LinkedIn link stored on the publication target.
  • Integration with core scheduling, retries, and per-channel publication status.
  • One-time, expiring OAuth state bound to the initiating Odoo user and account.
  • Actionable account health on expired or rejected authorization.

Setup

  1. Create or use your own LinkedIn Developer App.
  2. Open Social Marketing → Configuration → LinkedIn Settings.
  3. Enter your own LinkedIn Client ID and Client Secret.
  4. Copy the OAuth Redirect URL displayed by Odoo into the exact Redirect URLs for your LinkedIn app.
  5. Create a LinkedIn Social Account in Odoo and choose Connect LinkedIn.

The OAuth Redirect URL is generated from your installation's standard Odoo web.base.url. Make sure that value is your public HTTPS Odoo address before configuring the LinkedIn Developer App. No Automatify production callback URL or provider credential is bundled with this addon.

Requirements and external service

Requires Social Publisher and a LinkedIn Developer App owned or authorized by the installing organization. The app must have the LinkedIn products and permissions required for the selected author mode. LinkedIn controls API access, approval, limits, and availability.

Client credentials and OAuth tokens are not bundled with this addon. The connector communicates directly with LinkedIn's official OAuth, Images, and Posts API endpoints. Post text and selected image bytes are sent to LinkedIn only when publishing or when a scheduled post becomes due.
