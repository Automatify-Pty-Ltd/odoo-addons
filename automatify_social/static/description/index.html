Social Publisher

Open social publishing workflow for Odoo 19 Community

Manage social accounts and posts in Odoo with native scheduling, per-channel targets, retries, multi-company isolation, and provider-neutral connector hooks.

Core features

  • Social Accounts and Social Posts with Odoo-native forms and lists.
  • Draft, scheduled, processing, published, failed, and cancelled states.
  • Publish now, schedule, retry failed channels, cancel, and reset to draft.
  • Five-minute scheduled publishing cron with bounded batches.
  • Per-account publication targets for multi-channel fan-out.
  • Company-aware record rules and cross-company target validation.
  • Odoo-native extension hooks so installed connector addons can add networks without changing the core.
  • Per-post publication serialization to reduce duplicate writes from concurrent manual and scheduled attempts.

External services

This core addon makes no external network requests and includes no provider credentials. Network-specific connector addons may communicate with official third-party APIs and require credentials supplied by the installing organization.
